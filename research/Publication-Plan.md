## Combined contribution

Working title: **Bounded Evidence Sharing and Verified Local Interpretation for Collaborative Threat Detection**.

The argument follows the same evidence record through source policy, durable release accounting, provenance-aware correlation, recipient authorization, finite claim admission, correction/withdrawal invalidation and controlled report rendering. Disclosure remains source-controlled. Interpretation remains downstream of the security decision. A model cannot expand permissions, change release budgets or execute advice.

The recorded studies remain distinct:

- The 1,000-record deterministic protocol study measures sharing and declared-rule outcomes across synthetic organizations and controlled policy conditions.
- The eight-case Qwen3-4B CPU pilot measures finite claim support, lifecycle failures and generation costs on constructed authorized snapshots.
- No experiment measures the whole production path or analyst usefulness. The combined paper states this explicitly and retains the all-fallback negative result.
- CIC-IDS2017 replay is not completed. Laya, CDMs and compatible local task engines remain future evaluation options; no implementation or result is added by combining manuscripts.
